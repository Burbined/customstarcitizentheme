## Static stations — added to the plan from 7 Aug

Cheapest points available. 3×/week, on easy or rest days. Does not load the calves and does
not compete with running recovery, so it slots in anywhere.

Current (30 Aug): **47 push-ups, 35 sit-ups.** Note 45 → 47 gained zero points — the table
steps at 48, so one more rep is worth +1.

**Push-ups** — 5 sets of 28, 90 s rest. Add one rep per set per week. Never train to failure
more than once a fortnight.

**Sit-ups** — 5 sets of 25, 60 s rest, **plus one all-out 1-minute set weekly.** Sit-ups respond faster than push-ups
because the limiter is pacing and technique, not strength. Practise at test cadence rather
than sprinting the first 20 and stalling — same fault as your 2.4 km pacing.

**Re-test both every 3 weeks**, same day as a rest day, and log the numbers.

**This is the work for the A-Level block.** Oct–Nov is dead time for run progression — you
cannot build aerobic fitness while holding volume flat. But static station work is low
fatigue, needs 15 minutes, and can progress right through the exam period. That's where a
large share of your remaining points should come from.

Confirm the current test format (1-minute vs 2-minute windows per station) before building
the sets around it.

## Heart rate zones (observed HRmax 204, measured 14 Aug)

| Zone | HR | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Easy** | settle **145–155**, drift to 165 OK, ceiling **170** | Hold pace, let HR rise. Never slow down to defend a number. |
| Steady / long | 155–165 | Long runs, back half |
| Tempo | 175–185 | Phase 2 onward |
| Interval | 188–198 | Reps only — **HR must fall below 150 before the next rep starts** |

The recovery rule is the one that makes intervals into intervals. If HR won't come down,
the session is over — walk the rest.

**The flat 150 easy cap is retired** (9 Aug). In Singapore heat, drift is worth 15–25 bpm over
half an hour, so holding 150 late forces a walk — and walking has no flight phase, so it does
not build the durability Phase 1 exists to build. Talk test governs: a full spoken sentence
should be comfortable.

**Cadence: 162–165 spm** (measured 154–158 on 14 Aug). Count foot strikes for 15 s — 41, not
39. Shorter and quicker, don't reach for stride. Reduces forefoot braking, so it helps the
calves and economy together. ~170 later, not yet.

## Warmup protocol — non-negotiable, every run

1. 5 min walk → very slow jog
2. 2×15 straight-leg calf raises (gastroc)
3. 2×15 **bent-knee** calf raises (soleus) ← the one you're skipping, and the one that
   matters most for running
4. 4×20 m strides, building
Total ~10 min. No run starts without this. No exceptions on "short" runs.

## Calf conditioning — 3×/week, on easy or rest days

- Straight-leg calf raise 3×12, slow (3 s down)
- Seated / bent-knee calf raise 3×15
- Progress: double-leg → single-leg → weighted, over ~4 weeks

**Stop rule:** ache is acceptable and expected. Cramping, seizing, or twitching under load
means **stop the session and walk home.** Do not run through a cramp — that's how a strain
happens, and a strain in September costs you the whole plan.
