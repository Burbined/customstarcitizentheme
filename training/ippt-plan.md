## Weekly template

**Phase 1 (4 runs/wk):** Easy · Easy + strides · Rest/calf work · Easy · Squash *or* Long
easy · Rest

**Phase 2 (5 runs/wk):** Easy · Intervals · Easy · Tempo · Rest · Long · Easy

**Phase 3 (3 runs/wk):** Easy · Short quality (½ volume) · Long-ish easy. Everything else rest.

Hard/easy rules, enforced:
- **Squash is a hard session.** Never the day before or after a quality run. Squash day →
  next day is easy or rest.
- Never two hard days back to back. Time trial, intervals, tempo, and squash are all hard.
- Minimum 2 easy days between any two hard sessions in Phase 1.

## Heart rate zones (from observed HRmax 200)

| Zone | HR | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Easy** | **≤150** | Hard cap. If that means 8:00/km, you run 8:00/km. |
| Steady / long | ≤160 | Long runs only, back half |
| Tempo | 172–182 | Phase 2 onward |
| Interval | 185–195 | Reps only — **HR must fall below 150 before the next rep starts** |

The recovery rule is the one that makes intervals into intervals. If HR won't come down,
the session is over — walk the rest.

## Warmup protocol — non-negotiable, every run

1. 5 min walk → very slow jog
2. 2×15 straight-leg calf raises (gastroc)
3. 2×15 **bent-knee** calf raises (soleus) ← the one you're skipping, and the one that
   matters most for running
4. 4×20 m strides, building
Total ~10 min. No run starts without this. No exceptions on "short" runs.

## Calf conditioning — 3×/week, on easy or rest days

- Straight-leg calf raise 3×12, slow (3 s down)
- Seated / bent-knee calf raise 3×15
- Progress: double-leg → single-leg → weighted, over ~4 weeks

**Stop rule:** ache is acceptable and expected. Cramping, seizing, or twitching under load
means **stop the session and walk home.** Do not run through a cramp — that's how a strain
happens, and a strain in September costs you the whole plan.
