**Lineup-drop price dynamics — how confirmation moves the gates:**
- **Star CONFIRMED starting + fit → scorer price typically SHORTENS** (number gets worse, e.g. +650 drifts toward +550/+500). Shortening can push the price *below the floor* → the gate FAILS on price even though the fitness/starting gate passed. A shorter price is the book agreeing the player is likely to feature; it eats the very edge you were chasing.
- **Star BENCHED or flagged (game-time decision / minutes-managed / limp) → scorer price DRIFTS OUT** (number gets longer, e.g. +650 → +900). This looks tempting but the drift is the market pricing in reduced/again-uncertain minutes → the fitness/starting gate FAILS. A longer price here is a warning, not a gift.
- Net: a passing gate needs BOTH the team-sheet condition AND the price floor to hold *simultaneously* at the live number. Shortening threatens the price floor; drifting threatens the fitness/start condition. It is entirely normal for no prop to clear both — that is the expected outcome, and it means NO BET.
