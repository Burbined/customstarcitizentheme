## (f) CLOSING-LINE-VALUE / LINE-MOVEMENT NOTE

**Direction of movement since open (Wed Jul 15 → Sat/Sun):**
- **Spain 90-min:** essentially FROZEN at +125/+130 the entire window. Sharp/steam did NOT push Spain's match price.
- **Argentina 90-min:** shortened from +275 (open) → +240–+260. Money came IN on Argentina — consistent with reported 58–59% of bets/money on Argentina three-way.
- **Draw:** ticked from +195 → +200 (marginal drift out).
- **Spain to-lift:** DK moved -164 → -160 → **-150** (Spain lengthened / got CHEAPER to back over time); BetMGM sat -165/-175. Argentina to-lift firmed from +134 → +125 at DK but FanDuel held +136.
- **Total:** Under 2.5 steamed from -135 → -155/-165. Clear directional money on UNDER / low-scoring — the most one-directional move on the board.

**Interpretation:**
- The **public** leaned Argentina (Messi narrative, underdog + comeback story) and that trimmed Argentina's 90-min price without moving Spain — classic sign the shortening is public-driven, not sharp.
- The genuinely sharp signal is the **UNDER steam** (-135 → -165): informed money backed a low-scoring, defensive final (Spain's elite defensive record — reportedly 4 goals allowed across 13W-4D since Sept; Argentina defended deep vs England). If you want CLV, the Under was the number to beat — anyone on Under 2.5 at -135/-145 already has positive closing-line value versus the -160/-165 close.
- Spain to-lift drifting to -150 at DK means a Spain backer got BETTER than the opener — mild negative CLV for early Spain money, or an edge for late Spain backers shopping DK's -150 vs BetMGM's -175.
